**Glow Goo** adds a **throwable glowing goo** for **Minecraft**.
It sticks to surfaces, lights up the area, and keeps the vanilla feel simple and clean.

---

## Features

- Throwable `Glow Goo`
- Sticks to floors, walls, and ceilings
- Adjustable light level from `1` to `15`
- `Shift + Right Click` lowers the light
- Highlights nearby placed `Glow Goo` while held

---

## Crafting

| Recipe | Result |
|---|---|
| `4` Glowstone Dust + `1` Slime Ball | `Glow Goo` |

---

## Requirements

- **Minecraft** `1.21.1|1.21.11|26.1.2`
- **Fabric** or **NeoForge**
